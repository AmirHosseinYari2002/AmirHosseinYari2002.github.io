abstract: "As machine translation systems improve, standard benchmarks increasingly struggle to expose meaningful failures, while both automatic metrics and conventional human evaluation can provide limited reproducibility and diagnostic value. The Last Translation Benchmark introduces a living collection of human-authored, peer-reviewed text, image, audio, and video examples designed to challenge leading translation systems. Each example includes handcrafted verification rules for concrete failure cases, enabling reliable and actionable evaluation as models evolve."
